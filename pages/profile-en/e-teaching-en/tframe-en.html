Theoretical Framework Development

A guided twelve-step laboratory for building the theoretical argument of your study: define the context and theoretical needs, explore and compare perspectives, select and justify, define concepts, align theory with questions and method, map the framework, then write, evaluate and report.

Core tool

Theoretical Framework Builder

Work through the twelve steps. Each step explains the concept, gives a film or television example and checks your entries with transparent rules. The tool explains theoretical compatibility but never chooses a theory for you, and it never invents definitions, quotations or references. Your work is saved on this device only.

      Academic References

      References (APA 7th edition)

      Original theoretical works are cited in the editions used, with original publication dates where relevant; the 2020–2026 priority applies to methodological and applied scholarship, not to the origins of established theories. Journal quartiles (Q1/Q2) and indexing are not stated because SJR and JCR classifications change every year; check a journal’s current status at scimagojr.com or in Journal Citation Reports. Theory profiles in the Explorer are teaching summaries: read and cite the original sources. The video tutorials in the attached file are practical guidance, not peer-reviewed research.

      About the Tool

      About this tool

      Purpose

      This platform helps postgraduate students, doctoral researchers and academics in the Cinema and Television track of the Department of Audio-Visual Production to identify, compare, select, apply, visualise, write and evaluate theoretical frameworks for theses, articles and research proposals.

      How it works

      The Theory Explorer, compatibility badges, documented relations, checks and suggested scores come from transparent, rule-based JavaScript that can be read in the source files. The tool does not use artificial intelligence and does not choose a theory for you. Templates only arrange what you enter; library summaries are inserted only when you ask, as a starting point to rewrite and cite.

      Privacy

      Your project, including concepts and the framework map, is saved in this browser’s local storage only. Nothing is sent to a server. On a shared computer, delete your data when you finish. You can export, import or delete your data at any time.

      Academic disclaimer. This platform supports theoretical reasoning, framework development, scholarly writing and methodological reflection. It does not replace academic supervision or expert scholarly judgement, and the researcher remains responsible for the accuracy of every definition, interpretation and citation. All cited publications, reference documents and images remain the intellectual property of their authors and rights holders.
