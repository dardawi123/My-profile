An abstract usually moves through five steps — context, purpose, method, findings and contribution — whether or not it uses headings. Whether to use headings is decided by the publication, not by preference.

Word Count and Compliance

The presets (150, 200, 250 and 300 words) are configurable examples only. The actual word limit, format and keyword rules depend on the target journal, university or conference.

About the Tool

About this tool

Purpose

This platform helps postgraduate students, doctoral researchers and academics in the Cinema and Television track of the Department of Audio-Visual Production to prepare, structure, write, evaluate and refine research abstracts for papers, theses, conferences and journals.

How it works

All templates, checks and suggested scores come from transparent, rule-based JavaScript — word lists, counts and editable templates that can be read in the source files. The tool does not use artificial intelligence, does not paraphrase, does not evaluate meaning and never invents findings: every statement in your abstract comes from what you entered.

Privacy

Your project is saved in this browser’s local storage only, so that you can return to it later. Nothing is sent to a server. On a shared computer, delete your data when you finish. You can export, import or delete your data at any time.

Academic disclaimer. This platform supports academic writing and methodological refinement. It does not replace scholarly judgement, academic supervision or editorial review, and the researcher remains responsible for the accuracy of the abstract.
