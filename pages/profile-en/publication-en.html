Research Overview

My research examines the relationship between media content, audiences, and cultural representation across diverse contexts. I focus on television, film, and serial drama, including soap operas, Latin American telenovelas, and Turkish dizi, as well as gender representation and the portrayal of women in media. My recent work extends to podcasts, emerging media, and the role of artificial intelligence in the film industry.

Research Interests

  • Television, Drama, and Film Studies
  • Audience Reception Studies
  • Gender Representation and Women in Media
  • Comparative Cultural Studies
  • Content Analysis and Media Research Methods
  • Podcasts and Emerging Media

  • Artificial Intelligence and the Film Industry
