Dr. Merfat Alardawi

Assistant Professor of Drama and Film Studies

This website presents my academic work in drama, film, and television studies, with a particular focus on audience studies, gender representation, and comparative cultural analysis. It brings together my research and publications, academic activities, teaching experience, and professional development, along with interactive tools that help students develop their research projects. Research Interests

My research focuses on television, drama, and film. I am particularly interested in how audiences receive screen media, how women and gender are represented, and how cultures compare. I also study podcasts and emerging media, and the role of artificial intelligence in the film industry.
