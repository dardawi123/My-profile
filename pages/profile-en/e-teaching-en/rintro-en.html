      About the Tool

      About this tool

      Purpose

      This platform helps postgraduate students, doctoral researchers and academics in the Cinema and Television track of the Department of Audio-Visual Production to plan, construct, evaluate and refine research introductions for papers, theses, conferences and proposals.

      How it works

      All templates, checks and suggested scores come from transparent, rule-based JavaScript — word lists, counts, citation patterns and editable templates that can be read in the source files. The tool does not use artificial intelligence, does not paraphrase, does not evaluate meaning and never invents studies, gaps, references or claims: every statement in your introduction comes from what you entered.

      Privacy

      Your project, including the citation matrix, is saved in this browser’s local storage only, so that you can return to it later. Nothing is sent to a server. On a shared computer, delete your data when you finish. You can export, import or delete your data at any time.

      Academic disclaimer. This platform supports research introduction planning, writing and refinement. It does not replace scholarly judgement, academic supervision, source verification or formal peer review, and the researcher remains responsible for the accuracy of every claim and citation. All cited publications, reference documents and images remain the intellectual property of their authors and rights holders.
