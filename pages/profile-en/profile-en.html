Biography

Academic Profile

I am an Assistant Professor of Drama and Film Studies in the Faculty of Communication and Media at King Abdulaziz University, as well as a film critic and academic researcher. I previously served as Supervisor of the Communication Department. I earned my PhD from Dublin City University, Ireland, in 2023, with a doctoral thesis entitled The impact of watching Turkish drama on Saudi women’s attitudes and perception patterns: An audience study. I also hold a master’s degree in Communication and a bachelor’s degree in Food and Nutrition from King Abdulaziz University.

My research centres on drama and film studies, content analysis, and audience studies, with a particular focus on the representation of women in the media. I examine the content and complexities of foreign drama through critical analysis and a range of research methodologies, including audience surveys and mixed-methods content analysis, to explore the cultural and social contexts of dramatic works and their influence on audiences. I serve as a peer reviewer for several journals in drama and film studies, including journals indexed in Web of Science and Scopus.

Alongside my research, I run specialised workshops in screenwriting, film criticism, and podcast production. I teach courses that approach television drama and film from a critical perspective, helping students understand the aesthetics, techniques, and cultural significance of visual storytelling while developing their own skills in filmmaking and screenwriting.

I founded the Student Film Festival at King Abdulaziz University to foster a community of filmmakers and storytellers and to encourage applied artistic production in media studies. My interests also include adaptation and hybridity in dramatic and cinematic writing, particularly in relation to the representation of women in television series and films. Throughout my career, I have sought to bridge academic research and practice, advance film and media studies, and support a new generation of filmmakers and media professionals.

Prospective Students

I welcome enquiries from prospective students interested in pursuing a Master's by Research or PhD in a range of fields, including television, film, and radio studies; media, drama, and soap opera studies; audience studies; gender studies; comparative cultural studies; and cultural analysis. My research encompasses both quantitative and qualitative methodologies, including ethnographic approaches. Additional areas of focus include mass communication, the role of artificial intelligence in the film industry, and the study of podcasts.
