    About the Tool

    About this tool

    Purpose

    This platform helps researchers and postgraduate students in the Cinema and Television track of the Department of Audio-Visual Production to select, justify, develop, evaluate and refine a research methodology, with priority given to qualitative and mixed-methods research.

    How it works

    All suggestions, consistency checks, draft text and suggested scores come from transparent, rule-based JavaScript — word lists, option metadata and editable templates that can be read in the source files. The tool does not use artificial intelligence, does not verify references and does not judge the validity of a methodology.

    Privacy

    Your answers are saved in this browser’s local storage only, so that you can return to them later. Nothing is sent to a server. You can export, import or delete your data at any time.

    Academic disclaimer. This platform provides research-methodology guidance and educational support. It does not replace scholarly judgement, academic supervision, expert methodological review or formal ethics approval. Self-assessment scores are tools for reflection, not validated measures of quality.
