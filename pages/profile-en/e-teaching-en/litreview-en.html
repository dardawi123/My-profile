Academic References

References (APA 7th edition)

The attached reference file — literature review tutorials and film, television and audience research articles — is the foundation of this tool. It is supplemented with established literature on review types, searching, appraisal, synthesis and research gaps. Items marked “Details to be completed” still need full publication details.

Journal quartile rankings (Q1/Q2) and indexing are not stated here because SJR and JCR classifications change every year; check a journal’s current status at scimagojr.com or in Journal Citation Reports. No Arabic-language source on literature review methods could be verified to the standard of the other references, so none is listed; the Arabic terminology follows common usage in Arabic research methods teaching. The tutorial videos in the attached file are practical guidance, not peer-reviewed research.

About the Tool

About this tool

Purpose

This platform helps researchers and postgraduate students in the Cinema and Television track of the Department of Audio-Visual Production to plan, conduct, organise, critically evaluate, synthesise and write a literature review that leads to an evidenced research gap.

How it works

All search strings, consistency checks, gap labels, draft text and suggested scores come from transparent, rule-based JavaScript — word lists, option metadata and editable templates that can be read in the source files. The tool does not use artificial intelligence, does not search databases, does not verify references and does not create sources: every study in your matrix is one you entered.

Privacy

Your project is saved in this browser’s local storage only, so that you can return to it later. Nothing is sent to a server. You can export, import or delete your data at any time.

Academic disclaimer. This platform provides educational support for planning, organising, evaluating, and writing literature reviews. It does not replace academic supervision, scholarly judgement, systematic review expertise, or verification of original sources.
